How iFrame Embed Works

Place this simple snippet on any webpage, portal, or LMS. The assistant renders in an isolated sandbox, automatically fetching only the specified organization's knowledge base. 

<iframe
  src="http://localhost:3000/chat/<ORGANIZATION_TOKEN>"
  width="100%"
  height="600"
  frameborder="0"
  style="border-radius: 12px; box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.1);">
</iframe>
Floating Bubble Widget

To provide a floating chat bubble in the bottom corner of your organization's website, simply include our standalone widget script tag: 

<!-- Include before </body> -->
<script
  src="http://localhost:3000/embed.js"
  data-assistant-token="<ORGANIZATION_TOKEN>">
</script>
